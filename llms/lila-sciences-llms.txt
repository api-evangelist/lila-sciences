# Lila Sciences

> Lila Sciences is an AI company founded in 2023 building "Scientific Superintelligence" — a scientific reasoning model trained on experimental rather than internet data, coupled to autonomous robotic laboratory networks it calls AI Science Factories, that generates hypotheses, designs and runs experiments, and learns from the results. It sells two offerings: Catalyst (an operating system for science that accelerates an organization's existing research) and Creation (a partnership model for generating discoveries, launching products, and founding companies).
